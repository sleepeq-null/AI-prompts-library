Copy a prompt, replace the [BRACKETED] parts with your details, and paste it into the AI assistant you use.

Before you paste anything: never include personal or identifying information about students or patients (names, dates of birth, record numbers, etc.) unless your organization has approved the tool for that data. Always review AI output for accuracy; it's a first draft, not a final answer.

Tips for better results

  • Give it a role and context. "You are a 7th-grade science teacher…" gets better output than a bare question.
  • Be specific about the format. Ask for bullets, a table, a word limit, or a reading level.
  • Iterate. Follow up with "make it shorter", "simpler", or "add an example".
  • You're the expert. Check facts, especially anything clinical or graded.
