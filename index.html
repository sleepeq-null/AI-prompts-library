Tips for better results

  • Give it a role and context. "You are a 7th-grade science teacher…" gets better output than a bare question.
  • Be specific about the format. Ask for bullets, a table, a word limit, or a reading level.
  • Iterate. Follow up with "make it shorter", "simpler", or "add an example".
  • You're the expert. Check facts, especially anything clinical or graded.
